How scoring works

Each of the 8 pulses places a target band at a random radius. A ring expands outward from the center at a steady, slightly increasing pace. The moment you lock in (click, tap, or press Space), the game measures how far the ring's edge was from the exact center of that band. A perfect center-lock scores 100 points for that pulse; the score falls off the farther off-center you are, and locking outside the band entirely — or missing the pulse altogether — scores 0 for that pulse. Your total is the sum across all 8 pulses, out of a maximum of 800.
